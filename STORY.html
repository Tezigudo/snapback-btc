🤖 The Story of Our Trading Robot 🤖

A 30-day adventure trying to teach a robot to buy and sell Bitcoin

📖 The big question:
Can we build a robot that buys Bitcoin cheap and sells it expensive —
without us watching it all day?

🎯 Chapter 1: What we wanted

We wanted to build a trading robot for Bitcoin.
Think of Bitcoin as a magical coin 🪙 that goes UP and DOWN in price every minute.

If you buy it when it's cheap and sell it when it's expensive, you make money!
But if you do the opposite... you lose money. 💸

A human can't watch the price 24 hours a day. So we wanted a robot to watch it for us.

🧪 Chapter 2: Our 4 Robot Friends

We built 4 different robots, each with a different idea about HOW to win:

🔄
Snapback Robot
"After a big drop, prices bounce back!"
❌ Lost money
📈
Donchian Robot

🤔 Sometimes works
💰
Carry Robot

❌ Lost money
🤝

❌ Even worse than one alone

📋 Chapter 3: The Lying Report Card

We made the robots take a test — we showed them old Bitcoin prices from 2022-2024 and asked: "How much money would you have made?"

📈 The test said: "Carry Robot is GREAT! ✨ It passed all checks!"

We were so happy! But then we did real math:

💸 The real math said: If you started with $100 and used this robot, after 2 years you'd have only $62.
You'd LOSE 38 dollars! 😱

Why did the test lie? Because the test only looked at the middle result, not the BAD DAYS. There were a few really bad days where the robot lost 47% in one month! 😬

It's like saying "I usually get A's at school" but forgetting to mention you got an F on the BIG exam.

🔧 Chapter 4: We Fixed the Test

We built a better test that checks 6 things instead of 3:

  • ✅ Did the robot win most of the time?
  • ✅ Are the wins steady (not just lucky)?
  • ✅ Did the robot ACTUALLY make money? (NEW!)
  • ✅ Would $100 turn into MORE than $100? (NEW!)
  • ✅ Is the worst day not TOO bad? (NEW!)

Now the test couldn't lie anymore. 🛡️

🧠 Chapter 5: Making the Robot Smarter

The Carry Robot kept losing during big price rallies. We added a trend detector 🧭:

🧭 New rule: "Don't bet AGAINST the trend!"
• If Bitcoin is going UP a lot → don't try to short
• If Bitcoin is going DOWN a lot → don't try to long

Test on the worst month (Trump rally, Oct 2024):

Robot versionTradesResult
Old Carry Robot38 trades−47% 😱
Smart Carry Robot (with trend detector)6 trades−7% 😐

The trend detector helped a lot! But still negative... 🤔

🔬 Chapter 6: The BIG Test (data the robot never saw!)

This is the most important chapter. ⭐

We trained our robots on 2022-2024 data. They looked smart.
Then we showed them 2025 data — data they had NEVER seen.

It's like teaching for a math test, then giving a SURPRISE test with new questions.
If you really understand math, you pass. If you just memorized, you fail.

RobotPractice score (2022-2024)Surprise test (2025)Verdict
Smart Carry Robot 💰 +74.66% 🎉 −5.37% 💸 ❌ Just memorized
Donchian Robot 📈 +213.47% 🚀 −5.50% 💸 ❌ Just memorized
Funding Momentum Robot −66.56% not tested ❌ Bad idea
😢 Sad truth: Both "great" robots failed on data they hadn't seen before. They were memorizing, not learning.

✨ Chapter 7: The Surprise Discovery!

We tried ONE more thing. We trained the Donchian Robot on different years:

Training yearsTest yearWhat kind of year?Result
2020-2021 2022 first half 📉 Strong DOWN trend +49% ✅ WORKED!
2022-2024 2025 first half 🌊 Wiggly, no direction −5.5% ❌
🎉 Discovery! The Donchian Robot ISN'T broken!
It works GREAT when Bitcoin moves in a clear direction (📈 up or 📉 down).
It fails when Bitcoin wiggles around (🌊).

🛠️ Chapter 9: What's Next?

We need to build a weather detector for Bitcoin ☀️🌧️:

🧪 The plan:
1. Build a small program that watches Bitcoin and decides:
   "Is it TRENDING today? 📈" OR "Is it WIGGLY today? 🌊"
2. When TRENDING → turn ON the Donchian Robot 🤖✅
3. When WIGGLY → turn OFF the Donchian Robot 🤖❌
4. Repeat every day.

If our weather detector is good, the Donchian Robot becomes useful for real money! 💵

🎓 What we learned

  • 💡 Don't trust the first test. Always check with new data.
  • 💡 "Average" can hide BIG losses. Always look at the worst day too.
  • 💡 Most ideas DON'T work. We tried 7+ ideas, only 1 might work!
  • 💡 Negative results are still results. Knowing what DOESN'T work saves time.
  • 💡 Markets have weather. What works in one season might fail in another.
🌟 The big lesson:
Making a money-making robot is HARD.
But every failed test teaches us something useful.

We're not done — we just learned where to dig next! ⛏️
