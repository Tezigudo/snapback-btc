v3 results — adaptive risk gates

Three new gates tested as ablations. Most don't help; one helps a little; one hurts a lot.

The honest summary: v3 was an attempt to fix 2024 H1's losing window. The result — v2-strict is still the champion. None of the v3 variants beat it on total compounded return. Vol-regime gate offers a small "lower DD, lower return" trade-off. ATR stops are a regression. Distance-from-EMA at the tested thresholds doesn't engage.

Verdict: no-op at these thresholds. BTC's price wasn't 10–20% above EMA(200) at the moments our entry signals fired. To engage at all we'd need an extremely tight threshold (5% or less), which would also reject legitimate dip-buys in normal uptrends. Net: this gate doesn't separate good from bad trades for THIS strategy at THESE windows. Drop.

ATR-based stops (replace fixed 1.5% with k × ATR)

ConfigCompoundedvs v2-strictWorst window
atr_sl_k=1.5, atr_tp_k=3.0−21.84%−93pp−23.79% (2025H1)
Verdict: catastrophic. The idea was "widen stops in vol — fewer unfair stop-outs". The reality: when ATR is high, the bot also sizes positions for the WIDER stop (qty = risk / sl_distance, so qty shrinks when sl_distance grows). But the TP also grows. So winners are bigger but losers are bigger too — and the strategy's win rate isn't high enough to make the bigger losers worth it. The fixed 1.5% / 3% was better. Drop.

Read this: v2-strict already mitigated 2024 H1 (−12.56% → −5.58%) at no cost — it improved every window. The further fixes in v3 either don't engage (dist-EMA), trade total return for incremental DD reduction (vol-gate), or hurt badly (ATR stops).

The clean conclusion: v2-strict is the optimal version of the strategy in this codebase. There is no clean technical fix for 2024 H1 that doesn't pay for itself in losses on other windows.

4. Recommendation

For deploy: stick with multifactor-v2-strict.

It's the best total compounded return (+71.52%), 5 of 6 windows positive, smallest worst-case DD across the tested set, and already gives most of the 2024 H1 improvement v3 was reaching for.

v3 is shelved, not deployed. The code stays in strategy/signals_multifactor_v3.py for reference and future experimentation, but it doesn't go to production.

What v3 taught us:
  • The 2024 H1 underperformance is structural to the strategy class, not a tunable knob
  • ATR-based stops fundamentally change the win/loss geometry in ways that hurt this strategy's edge
  • Distance-from-EMA needs to be coupled to OTHER information to be useful — alone it's either inactive (loose) or noisy (tight)
  • Vol-regime gate offers a real but small risk-adjusted-return trade-off; could be worth shipping for a more risk-averse deploy variant later
If you ever want a "safer" variant with smaller drawdowns:
Use v3-vol-regime-only with vol_regime_max_pctile: 0.75. Expect ~+54% compounded vs +72% for v2-strict, but slightly smoother. This is a debatable preference — I'd default to v2-strict unless the drawdown matters more than the total return.
