🧮 The algorithm (each factor explained)

1. RSI(14) ✅ ON
Period 14 (standard). Asymmetric thresholds as you requested:
  • Long signal: RSI < 40 (oversold — but looser than standard 30)
  • Short signal: RSI > 70 (overbought — standard)
Why asymmetric: BTC has natural upward drift in bull markets, so being less strict on longs catches more opportunities; being strict on shorts avoids selling into rallies.
2. Volume confirmation ✅ ON
Require current bar's volume > 2.0 × SMA(volume, 20). So at least double the recent average.
Why: Real moves come with real volume. Tiny-volume RSI extremes are noise that reverts quickly. The 2.0× threshold was empirically the sweet spot — 1.5× too loose (false signals), 2.5× too strict (no trades).
3. Trend filter (EMA 200 on 15m) ✅ ON
  • Only long if close > 200-period EMA (uptrend)
  • Only short if close < 200-period EMA (downtrend)
Why: "Trade with the trend, fade extremes within it." 200 EMA on 15m = roughly 2-day moving average. This filter killed the carry strategy's biggest failure mode (shorting into rallies).
4. Funding sentiment proxy ✅ ON
Refuse new trades if funding rate is extreme (|funding| > 0.05% per 8h) in the direction we want to trade.
  • Won't open new LONG if funding rate > +0.05% (already crowded long)
  • Won't open new SHORT if funding rate < −0.05% (already crowded short)
Why: Without a real news API, we use funding rate as a sentiment proxy. Extreme funding = crowded trade = vulnerable to squeeze. Avoid being the last to join. (Source: Phemex, CoinMarketCap funding-rate dashboards, May 2026 research.)
5. Candlestick patterns (engulfing, hammer) ❌ OFF
Tested but DISABLED in production combo.
Why off: On 15m bars, engulfing patterns are too rare. Combined with all the AND filters above, requiring engulfing produced ZERO trades over 6 months. Research suggests engulfing works better on daily/4h bars, not 15m. Left in code for future use on higher timeframes.
6. MACD histogram agreement ❌ OFF
Tested but DISABLED.
Why off: Same reason — combining MACD with all other filters cut trade frequency to near zero. MACD adds redundant information when we already have trend + RSI. Available via require_macd=True flag if you want to experiment.
7. Risk geometry (stops & targets) ✅ ON
  • Stop loss: 1.5% adverse → close trade
  • Take profit: 3.0% favourable → close trade (2:1 reward/risk)
  • Max hold: 14 days (your request — was 30 days, now 14)
  • Adverse trend exit: if price crosses to wrong side of 200 EMA, close immediately
Why 2:1: Need win rate > 33% to break even. Actual win rate: 30%. Just barely above the line.
